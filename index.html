about

I am a visual artist

The main concepts around my work are sustainability, rituality, tradition, and playfulness. I create abstract forms combining colour, shapes, and rhythm. With “the seriousness of a child at play,” I choose leftover pieces of fabric and other materials, reclaiming what others deem waste, and transform them into a language of renewal, softness, and resilience - creating new forms in which vulnerability itself becomes a form of resistance.

Tenderscapes

The series of artworks revolves around the tension between softness and hardness and contemplates both the toll and the value of staying “soft” in a world that keeps hardening.

Nature Folk Kitsch

Trying to identify the connection between nature, folk culture and kitsch culture. Digital assemblages of digital and physical artworks.
