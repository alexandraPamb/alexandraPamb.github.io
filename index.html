I am a visual artist

The main concepts around my work are sustainability, rituality, tradition, and playfulness. I create abstract forms combining colour, shapes, and rhythm. With “the seriousness of a child at play,” I choose leftover pieces of fabric and other materials, reclaiming what others deem waste, and transform them into a language of renewal, softness, and resilience - creating new forms in which vulnerability itself becomes a form of resistance.

Ό,τι θυμόμαστε μένει | solo exhibition

"The process begins with the creation of a semi-fictional, colorful herbarium. With the seriousness of a child at play, beads, old pieces of fabric and threads are gathered. The design is transferred. The fabric is cut, sewn, and selectively padded. Gestures are repeated as time slows down, liberated from the imperatives of productivity. Neglected materials gain new lives and the tactile experience reactivates, through memory, fragments of a world that has passed and of a future that never came to be". Excerpt from Marina Demosthenous' text on the exhibition.
